What a trustworthy code comment should preserve

Source result means the identified passage supports the stated mechanism within its assumptions. Derived result means the handbook performs additional algebra or extends a model; cite it as a derivation and preserve the premises. Foundation with a reference gap identifies a background law lacking a verified locator in the supplied PDFs. Proposed policy is our engineering contract, not a rule authored or proven in a textbook.

Do not paste a source citation onto a broader claim: a centroid equation does not prove post-layout offset, a Black-law fit does not establish a universal current limit, and a classifier score does not establish yield. Numerical limits still need the actual PDK/model revision and design requirement. Physical PDF positions are one-based; printed page numbers are included only where checked. File hashes in the JSON and source inventory disambiguate editions.

The records below are curated reusable justifications, not a claim that every sentence of the handbook is a source theorem. Existing Philis implementation observations were excluded from this theory audit.
