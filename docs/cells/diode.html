Diode — shape catalog diagrams

Companion to docs/cells/diode.md. Sources: Hastings AOAL ch11 (diodes), ch14 (ESD). All views share the palette below. Junction diodes are vertical-current devices — see view (f).

(a) Square P+/N-well junction diode — top view

P+ anode island centered in an N-well; N+ cathode ring contacts the well. Square (max area/perimeter) geometry is the matching-preferred shape [Hastings 11.3 Rule 6]. Doc §1, §2, §6.

(b) Interdigitated stripe ESD diode — top view

Alternating anode/cathode fingers: minimum series R (≤3 Ω for 2 kV HBM) and maximum junction periphery. P+ strips are drawn outermost so all N+ fingers conduct uniformly [Hastings 11.2.1]. Hatched end strips = optional dummies for matched arrays. Doc §1, §2, §3.

(c) Waffle diode — top view

3×3 array of small P+ anode islands in a common N+ cathode plane inside one N-well: periphery scales with island count at fixed anode area (tunable A/P), and the array doubles as a unit-device formation [Hastings 11.3 Rule 5 — arrays of moderate devices]. Doc §2, §3.

(d) Schottky diode — top view, mandatory guard ring

Silicide barrier over lightly doped N moat. The P+ field-relief guard ring is mandatory around the Schottky contact edge — it eliminates edge field intensification; breakdown becomes the PSD/N-well avalanche [Hastings 11.1.3, 11.2.3]. Elongated contact + surrounding cathode contacts mitigate the high CMOS series R. Doc §1, §5.

(e) Orientation chart — stripe diode under the 8 dihedral transforms

A stripe diode with distinct anode (top, met1 stub + chirality dot) and cathode ends. All 8 transforms are DRC-legal for a standalone junction diode; for matched pairs with asymmetric layouts, devices must be superimposable by translation only [doc §4; Hastings 11.3 Rules 7-8; GENERATOR_MODULARITY §2.3]. R90-family swaps the bbox aspect.

(f) 3D isometric cutaway — P+/N-well diode (vertical current)

Front half of the die removed at the anode centerline. Forward current crosses the P+/N-well junction vertically, spreads through the well, and returns to the surface N+ cathode ring — this vertical path is why junction diodes get a 3D view. 30° isometric projection. Doc §1.
