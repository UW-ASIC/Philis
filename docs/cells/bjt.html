(a) Vertical NPN — top view, concentric C-B-E ring order

Collector ring outermost, base ring, square emitter innermost (CBE) — packs emitters of matched devices closest for thermal matching [Hastings ch10 §10.2.3]. Tank and NBL dashed; deep-N+ sinker under the collector ring's right edge.

(b) Lateral PNP — top view, circular/annular with field plate

Central minimum-size circular emitter (base diffusion), annular collector (base diffusion), neutral N-epi base between them. Met1 field plate tied to the emitter covers the whole base gap and overlaps the collector 2–3 µm — REQUIRED [PNR_ANALOG 6.A.00; Hastings ch9 §9.2.3]. Radially symmetric → orientation-immune.

(c) 8:1 ratioed pair — 3×3 "eight around one"

Center unit = 1× device QB; the 8 surrounding identical units form the 8× device QA. Spans 1/3 the length of a linear array → ~10× lower quadratic gradient residue [Hastings ch10 fig 10.24B; PNR_ANALOG 4.A.2]. Shared tank: each NPN needs its deep-N+ plug [PNR_ANALOG 6.A.0].

(d) Ratioed quad 4:1:1:4

Two 4× devices (Q1, Q4) and two 1× devices (Q2, Q3): ΔVBE = VT·ln(4·4/1·1) = VT·ln(16) = 72 mV [Hastings ch10 Eq 10.14]. Cross-coupled 2×5 unit grid — centroids of all four devices coincide, cancelling linear thermal and stress gradients. Hatched columns = dummy units at the array edges (§5).

(e) Orientation chart — 8 dihedral transforms

An asymmetric stripe-emitter NPN (emitter stripe offset left, base contact top-left) under the 8 dihedral transforms. All 8 are DRC-legal for BJTs, but matched devices must be superimposable by translation only — mixing transforms inside a matched group introduces misalignment- and stress-asymmetry [Hastings ch10/ch13; PNR_ANALOG 9.A.3/9.A.5]. Right: circular lateral PNP — identical under all 8 → orientation-immune.

(f) Vertical NPN — 3D isometric cutaway + cross-section (REQUIRED: vertical-current device)

Current leaves the N+ emitter downward through the P-base into the N-epi drift region, is collected by the NBL buried layer, flows laterally through the NBL, then up the deep-N+ sinker column to the surface collector contact [Hastings ch9 §9.2.1, fig 9.11]. Plan views hide this entirely — hence the 3D view. 30° parallelogram projection; depth axis = (+0.866, −0.5).
