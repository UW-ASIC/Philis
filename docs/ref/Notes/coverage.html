Sources, coverage and evidence

This handbook synthesizes all 13 supplied PDFs into 30 teaching chapters and an 84-rule constraint catalog. Source companions preserve the provenance, assumptions, reading depth and paper-specific evidence behind that synthesis.

What was read, and what the notes claim

The corpus contains 2,710 physical PDF pages. The complete text was extracted for navigation and reading. Every source and every main book chapter is represented, with deeper attention to material applicable to automated analog P&R. The books were not all read line by line: chapter surveys, targeted deep reading and selective visual checks are distinguished in each source companion. No claim is made that every exercise, bibliography entry or repeated image description was independently studied.

The Hastings file includes 592 pages of accessible descriptions after its index. These largely describe figures and equations already in the book; the physical page total should not be interpreted as 2,710 pages of independent technical chapters. Source links use physical PDF positions because printed-page offsets vary across the scans.

The notes separate source findings, derivations made here, illustrative calculations, current-code observations and proposed extensions. Historical process dimensions and reported benchmark gains are not treated as current PDK rules or measured Philis results. References inside a supplied book are not automatically additional sources reviewed here.

How to assess the evidence

The subsequent semantic audit checks the notes’ theory and original citation occurrences against the supplied PDFs. It corrects source locations, equations, assumptions and attribution; it does not certify every proposition in every book or review engine implementation. Theory provenance separates source results, our derivations and proposed software policies, with reusable comment references.

Technical claims link directly to the relevant supplied PDF pages. Important extraction ambiguities were checked against rendered pages, especially superscripts, formulas, tables and diagrams. The source companions record those checks and apparent source inconsistencies. Equations omitted by extraction were not filled in by guessing.

All teaching diagrams are original code-native SVGs. They explain topology, physical mechanisms or mathematical examples; they are not legal PDK layouts, extracted simulation results or reproductions of the books' drawings. Individual SVGs can be downloaded from the chapters.

Foundry rule decks, calibrated mismatch/correlation data, compact models, extraction stacks, reliability limits, package models and circuit testbenches remain the process-specific inputs needed to turn this handbook into production constraints. The supplied corpus does not define one universal optimum or establish complete coverage of every modern process.

Known source boundaries and apparent inconsistencies

  • The supplied Pelgrom item is the four-page ESSCIRC 1988 paper, not the longer 1989 JSSC article.
  • The wire-sizing file is identified as the supplied 27-page manuscript; final-publication metadata or a DOI is not invented.
  • The nth-order paper uses 2n units per device for its stated construction; text extraction loses that superscript.
  • The DAC paper's N-dependent settling-frequency expression is distinguished from an ordinary first-order −3 dB pole.
  • The performance survey's runtime percentages are internally inconsistent; only the supported qualitative lesson is used.
  • Apparent equation issues in the centering, electromigration and Hastings texts are flagged with corrected derivations or explicit uncertainty in their companions. They are not silently propagated into engine recommendations.

Repository context and artifact validation

The handbook changes only ref/Notes/; it does not implement the proposed engine changes. These code observations belong to the earlier authoring snapshot. They were excluded from the subsequent theory/reference audit requested by the user and are not recertified as current implementation facts.

The notes include proposed experiments and acceptance criteria. They do not claim that new Philis benchmarks, silicon measurements or source-paper experiments were run. Artifact checks cover local links, PDF-page bounds, HTML structure, SVG validity/accessibility, script syntax and educational moment arithmetic; see the generated validation report. The delivered validator can recheck structural integrity after edits; the JavaScript example checks can be rerun with Node.js.
